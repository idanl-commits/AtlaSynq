FAQ

Questions teams ask first

What is AtlaSynq?

AtlaSynq is the AI operating system for growing businesses. We bring customers through ads, SEO, and outreach — then answer, qualify, book, and follow up — so your team can focus on the work that matters.

Who is AtlaSynq built for?

Growing businesses that want more booked customers without hiring a full marketing ops team — from service businesses and clinics to multi-location operators.

Will AtlaSynq spend my ad budget without approval?

No. Campaign changes, budget shifts, and publishing stay under your control. Configure approval thresholds so automation never becomes a black box.

How fast can we go live?

Most teams connect ads, calendar, and SMS in the first onboarding sessions. We walk through your lead flow on the demo and map a rollout that matches your team.
